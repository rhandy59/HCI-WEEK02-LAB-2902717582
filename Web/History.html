History of Yogyakarta

Yogyakarta, often referred to as Jogja, has a rich history that dates back to the 18th century when it was established as a sultanate in 1755.

The city played a significant role during the Indonesian National Revolution against Dutch colonial rule in the mid-20th century.

Yogyakarta served as the temporary capital of Indonesia from 1946 to 1948 during the struggle for independence.

Today, Yogyakarta is known for its historical sites, including the Sultan's Palace (Kraton), Taman Sari Water Castle, and numerous ancient temples such as Borobudur and Prambanan.

  • Founded: 1755
  • first sultan: Hamengkubuwono
  • Historical Sites: Kraton, Taman Sari, Borobudur, Prambanan
  • Role in Independence: Temporary capital (1946-1948)
