Yogyakarta City

The Heart of Java

Yogyakarta, also called Jogja, is the capital of the Spesial Region of Yogyakarta in Java, Indonesia

It is known as a center of classical Javanese fine art and culture such as batik, ballet, drama, music, poetry, and puppet shows.

Yogyakarta is also known for its traditional arts and cultural heritage.

It is a popular tourist destination, known for its cultural heritage, historical sites, and vibrant arts scene.

  • Country: Indonesia
  • Province: Special Region of Yogyakarta
  • Languages: Javanese, Indonesian
